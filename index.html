Proposed reference design

Seven agents run the hiring workflow. People make the hiring decisions.

Each agent covers one stage of the process flow, from negotiating demand to republishing at-risk requisitions. Agents handle forecasting, outreach, scheduling, evidence gathering and recovery. Hiring, rejection and pay decisions stay with a named person, and every action is logged.

01

Autonomy follows consequence, not convenience

The same agent can act freely on reversible, administrative work and still stop for a person when the action shapes someone's career or pay. Read the chart bottom to top: the higher the consequence, the more orange it gets.

Leadership
Agent acts on its own, audited afterPerson decides, agent prepares the evidence
The top row is entirely human. No agent in this design rejects a candidate, takes an adverse trust action, or sets pay. That is the line regulators draw for recruitment AI, and it is also what performs: AI-led information gathering with human decisions has been shown to raise offer and retention rates in field trials.

    Your data stays put

    Agents read from your systems of record and write back through them. By design, candidate data does not train shared models. Memory expires and is deleted when consent is withdrawn or the purpose ends.

    Every agent has an identity

    Each agent has its own permissions and its own trail, so any action can be traced to the agent, the policy version and the person who approved it.

    Built to be handed over

    Co-created with your team, documented as it is built, and designed for your ownership: playbooks, rubrics and thresholds live in your configuration, not in vendor code.
    04

    Inside each agent: the same eight-part build

    Every agent is assembled from the same parts: a goal, memory with an expiry date, a plan, guardrails on the way in and out, scoped tools, an autonomy gate, and a record. Pick an agent.

    06

    Red lines, held regardless of what competitors ship

    Some capabilities are left out on purpose. They carry legal exposure today and erode candidate trust, and the same outcome can be reached with evidence and a human decision.

    LeadershipLegal and privacy
    No emotion inferenceNothing is scored from tone of voice or facial expression.
    No auto-rejectA person confirms every exit, with an appeal route.
    No AI negotiatorThe agent never holds or bargains the pay ceiling.
    No hidden scoresNo cross-employer score gates a candidate; evidence, not a number.
